01 — ABOUT

A little about me.

My background combines mechanical engineering, management research, and data analytics.

I have conducted research across microfluidic engineering, digital-twin simulation, and supply chain risk. These experiences have shaped my interest in using rigorous quantitative methods to understand how people and organizations make decisions when information is incomplete and conditions change quickly.

My current research interests center on operations and technology management, including decision support under uncertainty, human–AI systems, and resilient supply networks.
